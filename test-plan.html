Airport Trip Planner: Test Plan

Use unit tests for the recommendation rules and contract tests for the adapters. After deployment, check the integrations on staging. Load, failure and model-quality tests are required before production use; local results do not establish those outcomes.

The six worked examples are also unit tests, with the same inputs and expected answers.

Target: every rule above covered; at least 90% line coverage on the planner rules package (rules code only, not adapters).

2. Contract tests (every commit, against stubs)

Stubs of the traveller service, venue service, offers feed and Bedrock return the shapes in Outside services and AI keyword reading. Each adapter is tested for: the normal answer, missing optional fields, a 404, a 500, a slow answer past the deadline, and malformed JSON. When a real service's owners confirm their format, their example responses replace the stub's.

5. AI quality (before go-live)

The labelled-set comparison in AI keyword reading: the model must clearly beat keyword-synonym matching on at least 200 hand-labelled venues.

6. Accessibility (before go-live)

Automated checks (axe) on every screen state in Screens, plus a manual pass with a screen reader (VoiceOver on iOS, TalkBack on Android) and keyboard only.

Done means

All unit and contract tests pass in CI; every staging test passes after the release candidate's deploy; load, failure, AI quality and accessibility results are written down with their dates. Real measurements replace the assumed targets in the AWS design.
